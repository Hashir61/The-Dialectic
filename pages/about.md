The Dialectic is a journal of essays in philosophy, politics, history, and literature. The essays proceed by argument rather than assertion. They are concerned with the structure of positions — what a claim commits one to, what it excludes, and whether it survives contact with its strongest objections.
The philosophical essays treat the problems of ontology, epistemology, and ethics as open questions rather than settled divisions of an academic curriculum. The political essays examine the justificatory structures of institutions and power. The historical essays treat the past as a site of argument rather than chronicle.
No position taken here is final. Every essay is an attempt, not a verdict.
