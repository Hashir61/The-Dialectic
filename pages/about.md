I'm Hashir Usman, and this is where I write essays on politics, philosophy, history, cosmology and literature — the questions I keep returning to, worked out on the page rather than left as opinions.

My interest in these subjects grew out of a mix of experience and study: serving as General Secretary for the State Bank of Pakistan's National Finance Initiative, completing a business foundations course at the University of Pennsylvania's Wharton School, and leading a finance team through a private equity case competition. Each pulled me toward the same questions from a different angle — what an institution owes the people inside it, what a good argument actually requires, and where the numbers stop being enough.

This journal is the space where I follow those questions further.
